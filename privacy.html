Privacy policy: gog-personal

Last updated: 9 November 2026

gog-personal is a personal tool. Its only user is Xavier Armitage, signed in with his own Google account.

What it accesses

With the user's consent, it reads, labels, archives and drafts Gmail messages, manages Gmail labels and filters, and reads and edits Google Calendar events.

How the data is used

  • Data is processed on Xavier's own computer, for his own use: sorting his inbox, reminders and drafting replies he sends himself.
  • Parts of messages may be passed to AI tools Xavier uses for that same purpose.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for their own use, or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the computer's system keychain.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
